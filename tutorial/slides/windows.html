The math · Peak, homozygosity, copy number

Windows turn weak single calls into a strong signal

Variant density

dw = homozygous candidates in 1 Mb window w

Peak: the tallest window, grown while the next window (or the one after) is ≥ 25% of its height.

Ours: 2,582 vs 125 per Mb on average, about 21× more.

Homozygosity

hw = homozygous sites / sites called

Sites with ≥ 4 reads and ≥ 1 new-letter read; a window needs ≥ 20. Averaged over 5 windows, then hmutant − hsibling.

A heterozygote with 4 reads looks homozygous (½)4 = 1 time in 16, in both samples alike, so the difference cancels it.

Copy number

rw = log2( Dmutant / Dcontrol )

D = depth in a 500 kb window ÷ that sample’s median depth, so sequencing more does not shift it.

One copy lost: log2(1/2) = −1. One gained: log2(3/2) = +0.58.

Why it works at 3×: each call may be wrong, but errors scatter evenly over the genome. Only linkage piles thousands of real homozygous variants into one region.

The genome-wide plots use simple arithmetic over windows rather than statistical tests. Variant density counts homozygous high-confidence candidates in each one-megabase window; the peak is the tallest window, grown along the chromosome while the next window, or the one after, so a single dip is tolerated, stays above a quarter of its height. Ours is about 21 times the genome average. Homozygosity is the share of called sites in a window where every read shows the new letter. At low depth a heterozygote sometimes shows only the new letter, one time in sixteen with four reads, which inflates the share in both samples alike, so the mutant-minus-sibling difference cancels it. The copy-number ratio first divides each sample's depth by its own median, so a sample sequenced twice as deeply does not look duplicated, then takes log base two of mutant over control: minus one means half the copies, plus 0.58 means one and a half times. The general point: single calls at three-fold coverage are noisy, but noise is spread everywhere, while linkage concentrates real signal in one place.
