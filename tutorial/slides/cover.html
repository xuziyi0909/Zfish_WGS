Finding the mutation: whole-genome sequencing of zebrafish mutants

How our pipeline turns raw sequencing files into a short list of candidate mutations, and how to read what it gives you.

One command · ./run_wgs.sh

About 2 hours of computer time

No coding needed

Welcome. This tutorial walks through the whole process, from the sequencing files the core gives us to a short list of candidate mutations we can test at the bench. It has five parts: the ideas behind the analysis, how to run the pipeline, what each step does, how to read the results, and a worked example using our own jaw mutant data. You do not need any coding experience; the pipeline is one command.
