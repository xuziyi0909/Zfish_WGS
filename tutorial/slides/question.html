This is the whole job in one picture. Any two zebrafish differ from the reference genome at millions of places; lab strains are very diverse. Almost all of that is harmless background. The pipeline removes what the sibling also carries, keeps changes that hit proteins, and ranks them. Even then we are left with hundreds of candidates, so the last steps are always biology and bench work: which gene fits the phenotype, and does the variant segregate with it.
