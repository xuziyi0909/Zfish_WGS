Orange = high confidence: these two categories feed priorities 1–3 and the homozygosity peak · 26

This table is the exact rule set. Two definitions matter: the mutant has a variant when at least two reads show it and the position has at least three reads; a control is covered when it has at least four reads there. In absent mode, any trace in the control makes a variant shared. In sibling mode the mutant must also show the variant in at least 90 percent of its reads, and the control may show it in up to 60 percent: zero control reads gives mutant-specific, a few gives mutant-enriched. Not homozygous is a sibling-mode bin for variants the mutant carries on only one copy: they cannot be a recessive mutation. With several mutants, mutant_rule decides whether one or all must carry it.
