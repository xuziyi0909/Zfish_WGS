Pick the comparison mode that fits your control

mode: absent

Control: an unrelated wild-type fish or line, e.g. the parental strain.

A candidate is: in the mutant, and in no control reads at all.

Also for: dominant or brand-new mutations.

mode: sibling

Our jaw mutant

Control: wild-type-looking siblings of a recessive mutant, one fish or a pool.

A candidate is: in at least 90% of mutant reads, and at most 60% of control reads.

New category: mutant-enriched (in some sibling reads).

comparison:
mode: sibling # then save, and run ./run_wgs.sh

Thresholds: min_alt_fraction_mutant 0.9 · max_alt_fraction_control 0.6 (both adjustable) · 14

This is the one setting that depends on your experiment. Absent mode is strict: the control must show no trace of the variant. That is right for an unrelated control, or for a dominant or brand-new mutation. Sibling mode is for the usual screen design: mutants versus their wild-type-looking siblings. It requires the mutant to carry the variant on both copies, in at least 90 percent of reads, and allows the sibling up to 60 percent, which covers carriers. For a mutant pool with a few mis-sorted fish, lower the 0.9 to 0.8. Changing the mode and re-running only repeats the last steps, about 20 minutes.
