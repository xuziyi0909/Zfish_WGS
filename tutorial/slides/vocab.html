Key words

Seven words cover most of the report

WordWhat it means
ReadOne short piece of DNA from the sequencer (here 151 letters long)
CoverageHow many reads cover each position on average: 15× is good, 3× is thin
Reference genomeThe standard zebrafish sequence (GRCz11) that every fish is compared with
VariantA difference from the reference: a changed letter (SNV) or a small insertion or deletion (indel)
HomozygousBoth copies of the chromosome carry the variant; heterozygous: only one copy
Allele fractionShare of reads showing the variant: about 1.0 if homozygous, about 0.5 if heterozygous
ImpactPredicted effect: HIGH breaks the protein (stop, frameshift, splice site); MODERATE swaps one amino acid

Zebrafish WGS tutorial · 5

These seven words are enough to read the report. Coverage is the one people underestimate: at 3x, a heterozygous variant is often seen in only one read, or none, so a single fish at low coverage can look homozygous by chance. Allele fraction is what sibling mode uses: it is simply the share of reads at that spot that carry the variant.
