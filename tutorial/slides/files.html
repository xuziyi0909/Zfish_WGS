Everything lands in the results folder. Two files are enough for most people: report.html, a single page you open in any browser, and the Excel workbook in mutant_specific. The tsv files hold the same tables for anyone who wants to work in R or Python. peak_candidates lists the protein-changing variants inside the homozygosity peak. The alignments folder has the BAM files you need to look at a variant in IGV.
