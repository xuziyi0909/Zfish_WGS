Back to the bench

Four next steps, in order

1

Confirm linkage

Genotype about 20 mutants and 20 siblings at 3–4 chr7 markers, e.g. HERC1 Q3550H, plus markers near 22 and 33 Mb.

2

Sequence the leads

Sanger the coding exons and UTRs of tmppe, TJP1, HERC1, cog4 and thap11 in mutants.

3

Test function

Crispants or complementation crosses; compare Alcian blue cartilage staining at 4–5 dpf.

4

Sequence deeper

15× or more, paired-end, pools of 20–50 mutants and 20–50 siblings, run in sibling mode.

A causal recessive mutation is homozygous in every mutant and never in a wild-type sibling · 51

The analysis points to a region and a few leads; the bench decides. First confirm linkage cheaply: genotype a few markers across the peak in individual mutants and siblings. If every mutant is homozygous at a marker and no sibling is, the mutation is close; recombinants narrow the interval. Second, Sanger-sequence the leads in that narrower interval, including UTRs, because cog4 and thap11 have no coding change. Third, test function with crispants or complementation. Finally, for the next mutant, plan deeper paired-end sequencing of pools from the start; it resolves the interval far better than one fish per group at 3x.
