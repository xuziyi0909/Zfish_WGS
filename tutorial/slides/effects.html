HIGH means the protein is probably broken; MODERATE means one amino acid changes, or a few are inserted or deleted in frame. A variant that touches several transcripts of a gene can have several effects; the tables show the most severe one. The right-hand table is how to read the protein notation in the tables. All four examples are real variants from our mutant: p dot means the protein, c dot the coding DNA, and the numbers are positions counted from the start codon.
