The homozygosity plot looks at the same question differently: not counting candidate variants, but asking what share of all variant sites are homozygous in each sample. The two samples track each other closely almost everywhere, as expected for siblings. The bottom panel is the difference, and its tallest positive block is on chromosome 7, at the same place as the peak. When two views agree like this, we can be fairly confident the mutation is linked to that region. The next step is to confirm the linkage in more fish.
