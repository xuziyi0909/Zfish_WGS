This is the clearest result of the whole analysis, and it is a region rather than a single variant. Counting homozygous candidate variants per megabase, chromosome 7 between 22 and 33 megabases stands out at about twenty times the genome average. That is the footprint of the linked region from Part 1: the mutation is most likely inside it. Forty-four priority one or two variants fall in the peak, so we still need the checks and the biology to choose among them.
