Matching sounds trivial but has two traps. First, an insertion or deletion inside a repeat, such as one extra A in a run of A's, can be written at several equivalent positions. Our caller always writes it at the leftmost one, but the Ensembl list often does not; without rewriting, 470,839 known indels would never match. Second, a position can carry several different changes. We require the same change, not just the same position, because a new ENU allele can arise at a site that is already variable in other strains, and it should still count as novel.
