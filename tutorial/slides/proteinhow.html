Here is the method behind the aldh1a2 example. For each missense change the pipeline takes the zebrafish protein of the annotated transcript and checks that the reference amino acid really sits at that position. It aligns that protein with up to four NCBI RefSeq proteins of the same gene, preferring curated ones, and with the closest Ensembl Compara ortholog in each of six species: human, rat, chicken, frog, medaka and fugu. It reads the residue lined up with the variant, but only trusts it when the surrounding twenty residues align reasonably well, so a poorly aligned region cannot give a false answer. The rules are applied in this order. Only the first two move a variant to priority 5; conserved changes are listed first within their priority.
