Example

slc39a13 A23P had the best jaw biology (mouse knockout: small jaw), but it is rs507894324, a known polymorphism. It is now flagged and moved to priority 5.

Columns in the tables: novel (TRUE/FALSE) and known_id (the rs number) · 26

Known variants are differences already seen in other zebrafish lines; they are ordinary strain polymorphisms. A mutation from an ENU screen, or a spontaneous one, is essentially always new. The pipeline downloads Ensembl's list of eighteen million known zebrafish variants once and labels every call that matches by position and by the changed letter. In our mutant more than half of the strongest protein-changing candidates were known. The best example is slc39a13: the mouse knockout has a small jaw, so it looked perfect, but it is a catalogued polymorphism.
