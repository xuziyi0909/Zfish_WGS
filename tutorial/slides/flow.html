Orange outline: steps added or extended after the jaw-mutant analysis · 19

Here is the whole pipeline on one slide. The first four steps turn raw reads into a list of variants for every sample; they are standard for any sequencing project. Steps five to eight decide which variants matter. Step six and step eight are new: they came directly out of the jaw-mutant analysis, where we found that several convincing candidates were really known strain variants or errors in the reference genome. Step seven now has the sibling mode. The next slides go through the steps in order.
