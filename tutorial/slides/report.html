Orange outline: where the candidates are · 45

The report reads from top to bottom. At the very top are summary tiles and a box naming the strongest genomic signal, the homozygosity peak, with the top variants inside it. Then nine sections. Check section one first: if coverage is poor, every later number is shakier. Sections four to six are where the candidates are. Gene names link to Ensembl and ZFIN, and locations link to the Ensembl genome browser. Section six has a Checks column that says whether each variant is known or novel and how its amino acid compares across species.
