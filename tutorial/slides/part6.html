The mathematics of variant calling

From reads to a VCF: error models, genotype likelihoods, Bayes’ rule, the bcftools caller, depth, and how our own rules fit in. Worked with numbers from our tmppe variant.

This appendix is for anyone who wants the full derivations behind the short math slides in Part 3. It follows one position from the reads to the VCF line: how the error of a single letter is modelled, how the reads at a position become genotype likelihoods, how a prior turns those into probabilities, how bcftools decides that a site is variant at all, and which steps are plain rules rather than statistics. Every formula is checked against our own data: the tmppe variant on chromosome 7.
