Why so thin?

Only one read file per sample was available, and 89% of reads ran into adapter: the DNA fragments were short.

At 3×, one copy of a variant is often missed, so every call rests on a few reads.

Ideal: 15× or more, paired-end.

Report section 1 · not recorded: whether each sample was one fish or a pool · 46

Our example. The sequencing run itself was excellent: almost every read kept, high quality, almost all mapped, few duplicates. The problem is quantity. Only one of the two read files per sample was available, and most fragments were shorter than the read, so a quarter of all letters were trimmed as adapter. That leaves about three-fold coverage, a fifth of what we would like. Keep this in mind on the next slides: every candidate rests on a handful of reads, and some heterozygous variants in the sibling will have been missed.
