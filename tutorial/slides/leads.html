After the checks, these are the leads inside the peak. tmppe and TJP1 are the only priority one or two novel changes in the peak at residues conserved across all aligned vertebrates: two of the 22 such variants genome-wide. HERC1 Q3550H has the best read support and human HERC1 loss causes facial dysmorphism, but the position is not conserved. The stop-lost variant is the only priority one but sits in an unnamed gene. The known jaw genes cog4 and thap11 sit in the core of the peak without a coding change, so a regulatory change is possible. Homozygosity mapping now ranks tmppe and HERC1 first: they sit in the best interval, where no mutant DNA carries another version. TJP1 and the stop-lost sit in the linked region, where about one mutant read in nine comes from another version, so if the sample is a pool they are less likely. None of these is an answer yet: they are what to genotype and sequence next.
