Edit with nano, or in Excel and save as “Tab delimited text” · 12

The sample sheet is the only file most people ever edit. Four columns: a short sample name, the group, and the paths to read 1 and read 2. Paths start with data/raw. The most common mistake is spaces instead of tabs between columns; the check command catches it and tells you exactly what is wrong. If one sample was sequenced on several lanes, join the lane files first, keeping R1 and R2 separate. Excel works too: fill in the four columns and save as tab-delimited text.
