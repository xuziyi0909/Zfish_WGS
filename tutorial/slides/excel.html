Try: Data → Filter · priority 1–2, novel = TRUE, allele_check = conserved · 35

The Excel workbook is where most of the work happens. The Likely damaging sheet has every HIGH and MODERATE candidate, sorted by priority; the README sheet explains every column. The columns on this slide are the ones to know; ortholog_residues next to allele_check shows the amino acid in each species. A good first filter is priority one or two, novel true, and allele check conserved: in our data that leaves 22 variants genome-wide, and only two of them, in tmppe and TJP1, inside the chr7 peak. The read-count columns let you judge the evidence yourself: 5 of 5 reads in the mutant and 0 of 6 in the sibling is much stronger than 2 of 2 and 0 of 1.
