To finish, the whole process as a checklist. Steps one to four take about fifteen minutes of your time; the computer does the rest in a couple of hours. Steps five to seven are where your biology comes in. Keep two things in mind: choose the comparison mode that matches your control, and treat the output as leads to test, never as answers. The written guide in the docs folder has every step in more detail.
